# ChillPup Ambient Audio Candidates — Human Listening Scorecard

**Task ID**: `CP-AUDIO-002A`  
**Purpose**: Blind or randomized human evaluation form for short procedural ambient-audio candidates prior to long-form production rendering.  
**Reviewers**: Minimum 2 independent adult listeners required.

> [!IMPORTANT]
> **Safety & Scope Disclaimer**: This scorecard evaluates sound character, unobtrusiveness, and looping continuity for owner-guided ambient playback. It does **not** evaluate medical, veterinary, therapeutic, or stress-reduction claims. Do **not** record claims that the audio treats, prevents, cures, or clinically changes dog anxiety or behavior.

---

## Evaluation Procedure

1. **Setup**: Use local candidate M4A/WAV files generated by `generate_candidates.py`.
2. **First Listen**: Listen to the full 3-minute candidate with focused attention.
3. **Loop Assessment**: Replay the candidate at least 3 consecutive times to assess loop continuity.
4. **Background Assessment**: Listen once as background audio during normal activities without concentrating on it.
5. **Playback Devices**:
   - Phone speaker (at low-to-medium volume).
   - Headphones / Earbuds.
   - Bluetooth speaker (if available).
6. **Player Handling**: Test pause and resume behavior in standard audio player.
7. **Break**: Take a brief break before listening to the next candidate to avoid ear fatigue.

---

## Rating Scale (1 to 5)

- **5 — Excellent**: Exceeds requirement; flawless execution.
- **4 — Good**: Fully acceptable; meets design direction without issues.
- **3 — Neutral / Borderline**: Slightly noticeable issue but acceptable; needs careful review.
- **2 — Poor**: Noticeable flaw or distracting artifact; revision needed.
- **1 — Unacceptable**: Immediate rejection (e.g. click, sudden volume jump, irritating frequency).

## Rejection Rules

- **Blocking Failure**: Any candidate with a score below the minimum requirement on a blocking criterion must be **REJECTED**. Do not average away a blocking negative score.
- **Audible Artifacts**: Immediate rejection if any reviewer notes a click, pop, abrupt volume transition, alarm-like sound, or startling event.
- **Obvious Loop**: Reject if the loop join point becomes clearly noticeable during three consecutive repeats.

## Optional Dog Observation Log (Owner Notes)

> [!NOTE]
> Testing with a dog is **not** required for candidate acceptance. If the owner voluntarily chooses to observe their dog during informal playback, follow these guidelines:
> - Test only in a familiar, calm environment at low initial playback volume.
> - Ensure the dog has free movement and can move away if desired.
> - **Do not test during panic, severe distress, thunder, or fireworks.**
> - Immediately stop playback if any negative reaction (panting, pacing, hiding, whining) is observed.
> - Record only factual observations (e.g. "dog stayed on bed", "dog walked to another room"). Do not record claims of cure, therapy, or anxiety reduction.
